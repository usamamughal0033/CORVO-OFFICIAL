How to order

Find a piece on the site or on Instagram, then tap "Order on WhatsApp" or email us with the item code (for example SH-001). We confirm it's still available, share the total, and arrange delivery. First to pay gets the piece.

Shipping & delivery

We deliver across Pakistan. Delivery charges and timing depend on your city and are confirmed on WhatsApp when you order.

Returns & exchange

Thrift pieces are one of one and pre-loved. Check the photos, size and condition before ordering, and ask us anything you want to know first.

Payment

Payment options are shared on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.
